// capabilities

You say what. Nope does how.

One sentence in natural language. Nope finds the number, navigates the phone menu, waits on hold, talks to the human, and gets it done.

🔍

Auto-Discovery

Give Nope a company name. It finds the right phone number automatically — no searching, no digging through websites.

🤖

IVR Navigation

Nope listens to phone menus, understands the options, and presses the right buttons to reach a human agent. Works in English, French, Spanish, German.

🎯

Smart Strategies

Built-in negotiation tactics: anchoring, competitor mentions, silence pressure, retention resistance. Nope doesn't take no for an answer.

🔒

100% Local

Your data stays on your machine. No cloud. No account. No tracking. You own every recording, every transcript, every result.

⚡

Real-Time Stream

Watch the entire call live — transcription, agent actions, negotiation moves. Full transparency via web dashboard or terminal.

🌍

Community-Powered

Add your carrier, your country, your language. Every contribution makes Nope smarter for everyone. MIT licensed, forever free.

// workflow

Three words. One call. Done.

01

You type

"Cancel my Canal+ subscription" — natural language, any language.

02

Nope calls

Finds the number, dials, navigates the menu, waits on hold, talks to the human.

03

You're free

Get a confirmation with full transcript. Average call: 4 minutes. Your time saved: 43 minutes.
